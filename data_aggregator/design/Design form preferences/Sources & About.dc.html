About — Desktop · 1280 · /about
What this is
A volunteer-run aggregation site for the 26 August 2026 Bhote Koshi / Trishuli flood. We pull every public data source into one place every 15 minutes, show official numbers side by side with their sources, and let anyone add what they know in under five minutes. Per place, we show how many people are believed there, how many are confirmed reached, and how many are unknown.
What this is not
Not an official source, and it must never be treated as one. Not a missing-persons registry — the Nepal Police and Red Cross run those, and we point you to them at the top of every page. We publish observed status only: no hazard predictions, no danger ratings for villages.
Why the numbers differ
Each agency counts something different: NDRRMA counts everyone reported out of contact; the Police count confirmed missing-person reports; MoFA counts foreigners reported to embassies; the Tourism Department counts tourists on agency manifests; the PM’s portal counts every report submitted, including duplicates. We show them all rather than choosing one.
