# 04 — What is blocking rescue

*Every proposed tool should be judged against this list. If it doesn't loosen one of these, it doesn't help. As of 2026-08-29.*

| Constraint | State |
|---|---|
| **Weather** | Search "temporarily halted" Saturday for rain and fog (Rasuwa CDO, via Reuters). DHM warns of heavy rain in hilly Bagmati/Gandaki. Langtang model forecasts: 5–24 mm/day, 89–100% rain probability every day through at least 5 Sep, peak Tue 1 Sep; 8–14 °C. Expect **morning-only flying windows**; monsoon persists into early October. |
| **Landing zones & fuel** | Helipads at Syabrubesi and Timure destroyed. Army flew ~24 drums of fuel from Kathmandu after ships ran low. Nepal has enough airframes (15 flying, 34 on standby); the constraint is LZs, weather and fuel logistics (ABC Australia). |
| **Roads & bridges** | All 42 km Betrawati–Rasuwagadhi gone; Betrawati bridge out isolates Rasuwa entirely. Mid-Hill Highway blocked at Trishuli; Kathmandu–Mugling open. 42 Bailey bridges requested from India/China. |
| **Comms & power** | Hydropower-fed grid dead; no way to charge phones. NTC flying generators and batteries by helicopter. **Starlink is not licensed in Nepal** (talks stalled June 2026 on foreign-ownership rules) — do not plan around it. China has flown 2 Wing Loong UAVs as comms relays and portable satellite stations on its side. |
| **Barrier lakes** | Two lakes upstream; the Chinese one rated "relatively high" breach risk. Every surge alert pulls crews 1 km back and stops tunnel work. China has a 5-person monitoring post; ICIMOD and DHM are the Nepal-side hazard channel. |
| **Tunnels** | Portals under 1.5 m+ of mud; worker positions inside unknown; adit coordinates reportedly unknown to some rescue teams. Excavators, drilling and controlled blasting; APF divers mobilised. |
| **Coordination** | Nepal declined foreign SAR teams (citing 2015) and asked specifically for tunnel rescue, transport/comms restoration, body ID/DNA, long-duration body storage, Bailey bridges. Arrived Sat: 4 PLA tunnel experts; an Indian Army/signals/Bailey-bridge team (reported as 11 or 26 members) landed at Syabrubesi and did aerial recon of Chilime and Langtang; a Korean 11-person government team + 8 Doosan staff with 6 hired helicopters. OCHA coordination team en route; CERF $2–2.5 M; no UNDAC/INSARAG. Local officials in Uttargaya: "the state was absent". |
| **Missing lists** | At least four unreconciled lists: MoFA Emergency Response Team (foreigners, WhatsApp intake), NTB (from agency manifests, signed 27 Aug), Nepal Police/district intake (name, age, last location, clothing), hydropower rosters via IPPAN — plus independent trackers with JSON APIs, hospital QR-code noticeboards and a community registry. Phones of the missing "not connecting". A 655-row PII table of the missing has circulated via a travel-news site — the standing exhibit of what not to build. |

## What was *not* found in any source (the gaps)

Telecom last-cell-location tracing; TIMS cross-matching against the missing lists; life-detection radar on the Nepal side; any ML in the loop; Starlink.
