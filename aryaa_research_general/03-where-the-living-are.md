# 03 — Where the living are

*Ordered by likely survivors per unit of rescue effort — the order a morning sortie list should follow. As of 2026-08-29.*

## 1. Hydropower tunnels and powerhouses — hours matter

Led by a Nepal Army brigadier-level tunnel-rescue cell with NEA and IPPAN. These are the only people in this disaster for whom the classic 72-hour clock genuinely applies. (Kathmandu Post 2026-08-29 "hundreds feared trapped".)

## 2. Cut-off but intact pockets along the gorge — days matter

- **Timure / Syabrubesi / Mailung hillsides.** Survivors ran ~500 m uphill into forest and were lifted by helicopter the next morning (~10:00 Thu). 123 airlifted from Timure (94 Nepali, 29 foreign); 900 from the three sites on Friday; 2,101 by Army since 26 Aug. Both helipads gone — a private helicopter turned back to Kathmandu unable to land. (KP survivor accounts; Onlinekhabar; Radio Nepal.)
- **Uttargaya Rural Municipality.** ~150 hydropower staff across the river without food; 40–50 in ward 7 who cannot cross; 10–15 students still waiting after 40 were airlifted from a secondary school; relief has not reached the village; ~3,500 sheltering incl. people needing oxygen. (Onlinekhabar; KP "washed-out bridges".)
- **Gosaikunda RM wards 2, 5, 6.** Ward chairs of Timure and Syabrubesi and the municipality vice-chair are missing; the municipality chair reports "nothing, nothing" in resources. Electricity is off everywhere, so surviving phones are dead — **silence is not evidence of death**.
- **Tamang Heritage Trail villages** (Gatlang, Tatopani, Thuman, Briddim, Nagthali) sit above the corridor with overnight lodges; no rescue reporting found either way — a genuine information gap.

## 3. Trekkers up the side valleys — safe but uncontactable

Langtang Valley (Bamboo–Lama Hotel–Langtang–Kyanjin Gompa) and Gosaikunda were not touched. Lodges have food; TIMS permits are mandatory and enforced here, so a registry of who went up exists. 420 tourists remain "out of contact" as of Saturday, separate from the 2,426; two Italian trekkers and their guide are known stranded above a landslide and receiving help. These people need a walk-out route or an airlift, not a search. (treklangtang.com; NxtImagine tracker; Inquirer.)

## 4. Kailash pilgrim groups at the border — recovery, not rescue

The largest blocks of missing foreigners — one operator's group of 55 (46 clients + 9 staff), another's 89 (77 pilgrims + 12 staff), a third's 17, and an 80-person spiritual-organisation group inside the Gyirong immigration building — were in the direct path at 07:00–08:30 and lost contact at the moment of impact. After three days no survivor from these groups has been located; the first Chinese rescuers found "nothing but ruins" (28 members of one group were saved by having stopped for tea). A pilgrim bus from Gorkha to Gosaikunda was lost at Dobhan; seven passengers jumped clear. ML will not change these outcomes, but reconciling these lists is what stops the "missing" number from swallowing the people who *can* be saved. (The National; Tourism Info Nepal; Tribune India.)

## Survival window

The 72-hour rule is for trapped or buried people. Stranded people with shelter last weeks — Sikkim's Lachen workers were airlifted on day 6 (2023); Helene's hollows were reached over weeks (2024). Hydropower camps have canteens; lodges have food. Day-3 urgency order: injured/hypothermic → anyone below the two barrier lakes or on unstable slopes → known-but-unreached groups → genuinely unknown.
