# 02 — The numbers, with their disagreements

*Every figure is `as of <date>, <source> reported ~N`. "Missing" more than doubled overnight Fri→Sat when hydropower rosters and foreign-national lists were merged — that alone shows the lists are not reconciled.*

**Implication.** With only 13 bodies recovered in Rasuwa and hundreds recovered 100–240 km downstream, the Rasuwa "missing" split into two very different populations: people carried away (recovery, not rescue) and people alive in pockets the state cannot reach. The second group is the target.
