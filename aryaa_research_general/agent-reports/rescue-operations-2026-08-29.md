# Rasuwa/Bhotekoshi flood — state of live-rescue operations as of Sat 29 Aug 2026 (~14:00 NPT)

*Raw research-agent output, edited only for personal data (roles instead of names; personal numbers removed).*

## Headline numbers (conflicting — see flags)
- Nepal Police, Sat 2pm: **626 dead, 2,426 missing, 4,451 rescued, 15,224 personnel** (NDRRMA). Of the missing: 933 linked to hydropower sites, 517 foreigners, 127 Nepalis living abroad. (https://nepaldisasterupdatelive.nxtimaginelabs.com/ ; https://nesraspace.org/floodwatch/rasuwa-2026/)
- NDRRMA Fri 3pm had said 538 dead / 977 missing — the "missing" figure more than doubled overnight as hydropower and foreign-national lists were merged (https://aninews.in/news/world/asia/death-toll-reaches-538-977-still-missing-as-rescue-teams-race-against-fresh-flood-threat-in-nepal20260828163314/). Wikipedia/AP still carry 1,924 missing. CBS notes local media floated "up to 12,000 unaccounted for", unconfirmed (https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nepal-flood-rescue-efforts-death-toll/).
- Tibet (Gyirong Port): 7 dead, 554 missing, only 2 rescued alive, as of 01:00 Sat (https://english.news.cn/20260829/97e4b4afda9c41988fbafbc1a8bac76f/c.html).
- Only 13 bodies recovered in Rasuwa itself; 233 in Chitwan, 158 Nawalparasi East — bodies carried 100–240 km downstream (https://www.mappr.co/nepal-flash-flood-rasuwagadhi/). Implication: the "missing" in Rasuwa are mostly either dead-downstream or alive-but-unreachable; the alive pool is concentrated in tunnels and cut-off pockets.

## 1. Where people are known/believed alive and stranded

**Hydropower tunnels & powerhouses (the main live-rescue front).** IPPAN counts 934 unaccounted across 11 projects; NA has done 19 tunnel operations; 350+ pulled out by Sat; govt still estimates 100+ trapped. Led by a Nepal Army brigadier (https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/08/29/hundreds-feared-trapped-in-hydropower-tunnels-after-bhotekoshi-flood). Per project (KP unless stated):
- **Upper Trishuli-1 (216 MW, Mailung/Haku)**: 254 rescued, operation ongoing. Survivors came out "covered in dark brown mud"; NA spokesman: "complicated to find the entry and exit points" (CBS). 3 airlifted from Mailung tunnel Thu (https://english.onlinekhabar.com/nepali-army-rescue-operations.html).
- **Upper Trishuli-3A (60 MW)**: 35–45 missing; cable-tunnel entrance buried. 82 personnel (62 NA + APF + Police) mobilised Fri night; two excavators clearing the portal; first controlled blast completed Sat; team reportedly reached as far as the trapped workers (https://english.khabarhub.com/2026/29/565520/). *Conflict:* PMO/Nepalnews/SCMP/Euronews say "350 rescued, ~100 still inside" and attribute it to **Trishuli-3A** (https://english.nepalnews.com/s/nation/350-people-trapped-in-upper-trishuli-hydropower-tunnel-rescued-alive/ ; https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/south-asia/article/3365555/nepal-army-races-rescue-100-trapped-hydropower-tunnel-after-flash-floods), whereas KP puts the 254 at UT-1. The NA unit is No.1 Second Movement Tactical HQ, Trishuli (https://risingnepaldaily.com/news/85645). Treat the UT-1 vs 3A attribution as unresolved.
- **Upper Trishuli-3 (37 MW)**: 213 initially missing, 85 rescued, site buried.
- **Upper Trishuli-3B**: 20–25 missing; a Chinese team was mobilised for this tunnel (Rising Nepal above).
- **Rasuwagadhi HEP (111 MW)**: 49 employees missing; a project official: "three to six people may be inside the six- or seven-storey underground [powerhouse]". 4 named workers rescued 28 Aug (https://risingnepaldaily.com/news/85697).
- **Chilime (22.1 MW)**: 8 missing, 6 believed in a mud-filled tunnel; the executive director is "mobilising a rock-climbing rescue team". (Other summaries say 15–20 — conflicting.)
- **Langtang Khola HEP (20 MW)**: 18 rescued, 42 missing, helicopter-only after bridge washout.
- Overall NA tunnel rescues: **191** (https://radionepalonline.com/en/2026/08/29/434948.html) vs KP's 350+ (different cut-offs/definitions).

**Timure / Syabrubesi / Mailung**: NA rescued 900 people incl. 56 foreigners from these three places Fri morning–afternoon; 2,101 total since 26 Aug (Radio Nepal, above). 123 airlifted from Timure alone (94 Nepali, 29 foreign) (https://english.onlinekhabar.com/rasuwa-flood-aug-26.html). The Syabrubesi helipad was destroyed and Timure's swept away — an Altitude Air ship returned to KTM without landing (NxtImagine tracker; https://english.onlinekhabar.com/rasuwa-flood-29-injured-rescued.html).

**Dhunche**: stranded pilgrims/tourists being walked out overland — vehicle to Mahabir, ~1 hr walk to Chaugati, buses to Kathmandu; Bagmati Province feeding them (https://radionepalonline.com/en/2026/08/29/434969.html).

**Uttargaya RM / Betrawati / Trishuli**: ~150 people stranded across the river facing food scarcity; ~3,500 sheltering in Uttargaya; electricity completely off, no way to charge phones; multiple ward chairs missing (https://english.onlinekhabar.com/rasuwa-cut-off-flood-shortage.html). Ward 7: 40–50 people can't cross; 450 sheltering at a Betrawati school; 40 students/teachers airlifted from a secondary school, 10–15 still waiting; "relief supplies have not been able to reach the village" (https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/08/29/washed-out-bridges-force-flood-hit-communities-to-rely-on-choppers).

**Langtang valley / Kyanjin / Gosaikunda**: trails above Syabrubesi are intact — no flood in the Langtang Khola; NTC has restored towers at Langtang and Kyangjin (https://english.onlinekhabar.com/ntc-ncell-telecom-flood-disrupt.html). Gosaikunda never in the flood path (https://travelhimalayanepal.com/news/gosaikunda-trek-2026-status-after-floods). Trekkers here are cut off, not in immediate danger; 420 tourists remain "out of contact" as of Sat, separate from the 2,426 (NxtImagine). Two Italians and their guide were "stranded upstream from a landslide but receiving help" (https://globalnation.inquirer.net/336015/the-foreign-tourists-missing-in-nepals-flash-floods).

**Tamang Heritage Trail** (Gatlang–Tatopani–Thuman–Timure–Briddim): several overnight stops sit in the corridor; no specific rescue reports found (https://www.magicalnepal.com/blog/bhote-koshi-nepal-flood-august-2026/).

**Tibet side (Gyirong/Kerung)**: 555 tourists and 499 residents evacuated, "no remaining stranded" per Xinhua; 80 Nepali container drivers stranded in Kerung (https://english.news.cn/20260829/3c732ff03b7547f097fc12b219f6c397/c.html ; KP above). An 80-member spiritual-organisation Kailash group (22 US, 12 Canada, 11 Australia, 9 UK…) was inside the Gyirong immigration building; no update as of Sat — first Chinese rescuers found "nothing but ruins" (https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/china/how-a-tea-break-saved-28-sadhgurus-isha-group-kailash-mansarovar-pilgrims-from-nepal-floods ; https://chinaglobalsouth.com/2026/08/29/chinese-rescue-team-nepal-china-border-himalayan-floods/).

**Security personnel**: 44 NA, 28–41 Police, 9–13 APF missing (posts at Rasuwagadhi/Timure), plus 15 customs officials (https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/44-army-personnel-41-police-and-nine-apf-personnel-missing-in-rusuwa-flood-95-23.html ; https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/08/28/nepal-flood-death-toll-reaches-469-as-hundreds-remain-missing-and-search-widens-downstream).

## 2. How rescuers are locating people
- **Helicopter visual sweeps + local knowledge**: authorities say ground reps "know where people are stranded" and direct sorties (https://thehimalayantimes.com/nepal/rasuwa-flood-security-forces-helicopters-mobilised-for-rescue). 16 helicopters flew 99 shuttle flights, 517 evacuated by air (https://sundayguardianlive.com/world/nepal-floods-latest-news-heavy-rain-swells-trishuli-river-rescue-ops-halted-evacuations-underway-272488/).
- **Thermal drones**: Nepal Drone Association (50+ pilots, thermal + LiDAR + loudspeaker drones; MoU with NDRRMA 15 Jul) (https://kathmandupost.com/science-technology/2026/08/26/drones-deployed-to-search-for-survivors-and-assess-rasuwa-flood-damage); NagarikAlert sent 5 thermal/cargo drones (2×100 kg, 1×50 kg, 2×15 kg), pilots flown in by heli (https://english.onlinekhabar.com/thermal-drones-rasuwa-flood.html); APF 6 drones + sniffer dogs (https://english.khabarhub.com/2026/27/565102/).
- **Tunnels**: physical entry, excavators, drilling and controlled blasting; APF divers mobilised. No life-detection radar reported on the Nepal side; China "plans" life-sign detectors and has 13 dogs.
- **Missing-person lists**: Dept of Tourism/NTB via trekking-company and expedition records ("out of contact" ≠ dead); Tourist Police 1144, NEOC 1149, Police 1155; US embassy consular email (https://theeverestholiday.com/blog/rasuwa-flood-2026-missing-persons-helplines-what-happened); Rasuwa DAO published a list of rescued persons (https://english.ratopati.com/story/76788/rasuwa-district-administration-publishes-list-of-rescued-people); hospital noticeboards + QR codes at Maharajgunj/TUTH (https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-29/nepal-tibet-floods-missing-persons-search-intensifies/107092492); unofficial community registry (https://flood.sodhera.com/). Ukraine sent an embassy team from India (https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/08/27/8050521/).
- **Not found in any source**: Starlink deployment, telecom last-cell-location tracing, TIMS-registry cross-matching, AI/ML. Phones of missing people "not connecting" (https://ianslive.in/havent-found-a-trace-families-search-for-missing-loved-ones-in-aftermath-of-nepal-floods--20260829122153).

## 3. What is blocking rescue
- **Weather**: SAR "temporarily halted" Sat because of rain and fog (Rasuwa CDO, Reuters) (https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/08/29/nepal-wants-help-for-flood-billions-of-dollars-as-bad-weather-hampers-rescue); rising Trishuli grounded helis (Sunday Guardian); flights "repeatedly disrupted" since Thu (KP 28 Aug).
- **Landing zones/fuel**: helipads at Syabrubesi and Timure gone; NA flew ~24 drums of fuel from TIA after ships ran low (https://english.khabarhub.com/2026/26/564912/). Nepal has enough airframes; the constraint is LZs and logistics (https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-29/nepal-tibet-flood-rescue-challenges/107089728).
- **Roads/bridges**: entire 42 km Betrawati–Rasuwagadhi road gone; 35 motorable + 45 suspension bridges destroyed; Betrawati bridge out isolates the district (Mappr; https://english.ratopati.com/story/76919/relief-in-bidur-chaos-in-rasuwa). Nepal asked India/China for 42 Bailey bridges (https://thefederal.com/category/news/india-sends-11-member-rescue-team-nepal-does-u-turn-foreign-assistance-255073).
- **Comms/power**: 87 towers down 27 Aug (60 NTC, 27 Ncell; NTC initially lost 152); Rasuwa 13/32 NTC sites down; fibre cut; NTC flying generators/batteries by heli; Ncell free voice/data/SMS 7 days (Onlinekhabar telecom; https://www.technologykhabar.com/2026/08/29/253011/). Hydropower-fed grid dead.
- **Secondary flood / barrier lakes**: (a) Nepal-side Lhende lake, 0.11 km², ~18 km above Rasuwagadhi, seen in Planet/Landsat by NDRRMA 27 Aug 11:44 (https://english.onlinekhabar.com/new-threat-rasuwa-lake-upstream.html); (b) Chinese-side lake at Chhochen Khola–Purepu Tsangpo confluence, >2.5M m³, barrier 150 m × 40–50 m, overflowing since Fri, "relatively high" breach risk, +3M m³ inflow expected to 30 Aug, secondary collapses during recon (https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1369273.shtml ; https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/28/china/china-secondary-disaster-flood-lake-breach-intl-hnk). Ops halted Fri for 90 min–3 hrs on both sides; crews ordered 1 km back (https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/searchandrescue-operations-halted-as-another-dam-collapses-along-nepalchina-border/12849546). *Conflict:* JoongAng says a "second dam collapsed"; Euronews/Xinhua say it overflowed and risk is "decreasing"; NESRA says one lake draining, a second larger one "under assessment". China has a 5-person lake monitoring post.
- **Tunnels**: mud-filled, portals buried, unknown worker positions; FM: "Nepal is new to tunnels" (https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20260829/nepal-needs-help-in-technical-areas-not-search-and-rescue-foreign-minister-says).
- **Coordination**: local officials report "the state was absent" in Uttargaya; Kathmandu–Mugling road open, Mid-Hill Highway blocked at Trishuli.

## 4. Who is coordinating; stated asks
- NDRRMA/MoHA-NEOC lead; NA (6,755), Police (4,473), APF (4,203) = 15,431 (Sunday Guardian). ABC's "30,000" and Radio Nepal's "6,700" reflect different scopes.
- Nepal **declined general foreign SAR** from India, China, US, UK, Japan, S.Korea, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh; asked specifically for **tunnel rescue, transport/comms restoration, body ID, DNA testing, long-duration body storage, and Bailey bridges** (Korea Times; Cyprus Mail). Rationale: 2015 coordination problems (ABC).
- Arrived Sat: **4 PLA tunnel experts** by heli direct from Kerung to a powerhouse; **Indian team** — reported as 11 (medical + tunnel) by Tribune, and as a 26-member Army/signals/Bailey-bridge team landed at Syabrubesi by NA Mi-17 by Khabarhub — did an aerial recon of Chilime and Langtang (https://english.khabarhub.com/2026/29/565524/ ; https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/world/11-member-indian-team-arrives-in-flood-hit-nepal-to-assist-in-relief-operations/). Korea: 11-person govt team + 8 Doosan Enerbility staff, 6 rented helis (JoongAng). OCHA coordination team arriving; CERF $2–2.5M; UN-SPIDER providing imagery analysis (https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/08/1168213). No UNDAC/INSARAG deployment found.
- China side: 2,141 rescuers, 86 specialists, 47 drones, 13 dogs, 2 Wing Loong UAVs for comms, portable satellite stations, heavy-lift drones dropping rescuers/supplies (https://english.news.cn/20260829/d711170c885442e7b50c7513afb17467/c.html).

## 5. Technology/data already in play
- **Copernicus EMS EMSR927** activated 26 Aug (https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/news/flood-in-nepal-emsr927/); **Vantor/Maxar** open-data release 27 Aug; **HOT + NAXA** tasking-manager campaign for NDRRMA, daily HDX updates, OpenAerialMap for drone imagery (https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Organised_Editing/Activities/Nepal_Floods_2026).
- **NESRA FloodWatch**: Sentinel-2 (27 Aug), 9 PlanetScope scenes at 62–93% cloud, 2 SkySat ~50% cloud, Sentinel-1 pending; 3,216 buildings/62 bridges in path; ~4 km of river cloud-obscured — "must not be interpreted as unaffected". Cloud is the binding constraint on optical imagery.
- ICIMOD/DHM/USGS: Planet identified a 600 m-wide collapse at ~5,200 m; DHM analysing Chinese satellite images; USGS reclassified the M5.2 as the collapse itself (https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/08/28/what-triggered-the-rasuwa-flood-scientists-piece-together-a-complex-chain-of-events).
- Existing flash-flood EWS sensors were tuned for monsoon floods and failed (Wikipedia).

**Bottom line for live rescue**: the highest-probability survivors are inside UT-3A/UT-1, Rasuwagadhi and Chilime underground structures (tens of people), and in cut-off but intact pockets (Langtang valley, upper Uttargaya, Dhunche). The binding constraints are cloud/rain grounding helicopters, absent LZs, buried tunnel portals, and an unresolved upstream barrier-lake threat that repeatedly forces withdrawals.
