# 01 — What happened, and the corridor

*As of 2026-08-29. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).*

## Trigger and sequence

- **26 Aug 2026, 08:37 NPT (02:52 UTC).** A ~610 m-wide section of glacier on the north (Tibetan) side of Langtang Lirung fell ~1,200 m from 5,200–5,400 m. USGS recorded the collapse itself as an Ms 5.2 seismic event — the signal was generated by the collapse, not a tectonic earthquake. (Wikipedia; EarthSky; Kathmandu Post "what triggered the Rasuwa flood".)
- The ice-rock avalanche dammed the **Lhende Khola** in Gyirong County, Tibet; the dam failed. The surge reached **Gyirong (Kerung) Port** — the China–Nepal trade and Kailash-pilgrim gateway — **seven minutes later**, averaging ~193 km/h over the first 22 km.
- It entered Nepal as the **Bhotekoshi** at Rasuwagadhi/Timure, hit Syabrubesi and the Mailung hydropower cluster, and continued down the **Trishuli** through Dhunche's lowlands, Betrawati, Bidur/Trishuli Bazaar and Devighat. The Trishuli rose **9 m in 30 minutes** at Galchhi (Al Jazeera; ICIMOD).
- Debris flow ran ~80 km; water in the upper gorge rose up to 80 m above baseline; **1.5 m+ of silt** now covers the main disaster zone. Bodies were recovered up to **240 km downstream** (Chitwan, Nawalparasi, Uttar Pradesh). A secondary Ms 4.2 landslide followed three hours later.
- Multiple downstream Indian states (Bihar, UP, Uttarakhand) evacuated along the Gandak system.

## Barrier lakes (live hazard)

Two lakes persisted as of 29 Aug, corroborated by separate sources:

1. **Chinese-side lake** at the Chhochen Khola–Purepu Tsangpo confluence: >2.5 M m³, barrier ~150 m long × 40–50 m high, overflowing since Fri 28 Aug, +3 M m³ inflow expected through 30 Aug, "relatively high" breach risk, secondary collapses seen during reconnaissance; water level reported to have fallen 10 m by 28 Aug. (Global Times; CNN; Wikipedia.)
2. **Nepal-side Lhende lake**: ~0.11 km², ~18 km above Rasuwagadhi, identified by NDRRMA in Planet/Landsat imagery on 27 Aug 11:44. (Onlinekhabar.)

Search halted for 90 min–3 h on Friday; crews ordered 1 km back. Outlets disagree on whether the second dam "collapsed" (JoongAng) or "overflowed with decreasing risk" (Xinhua/Euronews); NESRA says one lake is draining and a second larger one is "under assessment". China has a 5-person monitoring post at the lake.

## The 2025 precedent (same corridor)

In **July 2025** a supraglacial lake on the Purepu Glacier (36 km upstream in Tibet) drained down the same Lhende–Bhotekoshi line: 9 dead, 19 missing, Friendship Bridge and Rasuwagadhi HEP damaged. DHM had **no rain/river gauge at Rasuwagadhi**; Dhunche/Kyanjin stations showed nothing; there was "no mechanism" for Chinese warnings. Nepal and China agreed "in principle" (Aug 2025) to share glacial-flood data — never formalised. In 2026 the early-warning sensors were tuned for monsoon floods and the upstream stations were swept away before transmitting; a Stimson Center fellow called the region a monitoring "blind spot". (Kathmandu Post 2025-07-09; Climate Change News 2025-08-27; Wikipedia.)
