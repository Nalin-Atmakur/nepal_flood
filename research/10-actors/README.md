# 10-actors — who is responding, who owns what data

**Question:** Who is actually doing rescue/coordination, and who owns each data flow we might build on? Software for rescuers is useless without a channel to the people below.

**Method:** One entry per actor: role in this response, what data they produce/own, our channel to them (if any). Tag `EVIDENCED` / `HYPOTHESIS`.

**Contents**

- `actors.md` — government, security services, international, volunteer-technical, private
- `our-channels.md` — which of these we can actually reach, via whom

**Status:** populated 2026-08-29. Biggest unknown: whether we have any direct line to NDRRMA, Nepali Army, or a district EOC — see `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q1.
