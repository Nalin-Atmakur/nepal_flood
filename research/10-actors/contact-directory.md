# Response contact directory — Nepal floods, 29 August 2026

>A directory of the main organisations currently involved in the rescue effort. 

*Last reviewed 29 August 2026. This contains only public professional or institutional contact routes. `CONFIRMED ACTIVE` means the organisation is publicly documented in this response

## Nepal's Current Position

Nepal's latest stated position is that international support should answer **specific operational requirements identified by Nepali authorities**, not self-deploy. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs named tunnel access, technology to locate survivors, rapid forensic/DNA analysis and mortuary capacity as current needs. Reporting from the same briefing also identifies heavy-lift cargo drones for isolated settlements. An Indian tunnel reconnaissance team has arrived and Chinese rescue teams are being arranged, so generic rescue-team outreach is likely to duplicate work. [Official MoFA briefing](https://mofa.gov.np/content/1866/press-briefing-by-hon--minister-for-foreign/) · [latest needs report](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/08/29/nepal-has-not-rejected-foreign-rescue-aid-says-foreign-minister)

1. Get a named requirement and a Nepal-side sponsor from NDRRMA, the relevant response cell or an existing local partner.
2. Send a concise capability offer: exact equipment/team, payload or analytic capacity, deployment lead time, cost, licensing, data-handling terms and constraints.
3. Do not send survivor personal data, biometric data or graphic imagery by ordinary email. Offer a controlled link and ask what secure channel the authority uses.
4. Do not mass-email the list. Record the owner, time, reply and disposition in the shared team document so parallel volunteers do not duplicate contact.
