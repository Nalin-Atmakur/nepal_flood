# Direct-message micro-influencer shortlist

*Scenario plan, rechecked on 2026-08-29. This version contains named individuals only: no charities, foundations, news pages, institutional accounts or liaison organisations.*

## What "directly messageable" means

Every person below has a current public social profile through which an Instagram message request, LinkedIn message, Facebook message or public X approach can be attempted. This does **not** prove that private DMs are open, that the person reads the inbox themselves, or that a message will be seen. Check the profile from the campaign account immediately before sending. Do not put family information in a public reply or mention.

## Recommendation

Begin with **local, lower-volume accounts**, then one Nepal–India bridge and one diaspora voice. The response rate and audience fit will probably be better than starting with celebrities. A sensible first batch is:

3. Prasuna Thapa;

## Outreach message template

> Namaste [name]—I’m helping with a small, non-commercial Nepal flood project. With consent, it collects private last-known information from affected families, reconciles duplicates and publishes only coarse, non-identifying insights. We are not an emergency service or an official missing-person registry, and names, private messages, photos and exact locations will not be public. Before asking you to share anything, could I send you the one-page Nepali/English explainer for a quick credibility check? If it seems unsafe or unhelpful, please say so.

For a person who has already posted about the flood, add one genuine sentence referring to that post. Never paste a family story, name, screenshot, phone number or case detail into the DM.
