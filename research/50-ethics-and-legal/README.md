# 50-ethics-and-legal

**Question:** What are we not allowed to do — legally, and by humanitarian data-protection norms — and what could we do legally that would still cause harm?

**Contents**

- `DO_NO_HARM.md` — the specific harms this project could cause and the rules that prevent them. Read first.
- `data-protection.md` — Nepali law, humanitarian norms (ICRC Handbook on Data Protection in Humanitarian Action is the reference text), and the data-controller question. *Document, don't decide.*

**Posture:** this track has veto power. A feature that fails here doesn't ship, regardless of how useful it looks.
