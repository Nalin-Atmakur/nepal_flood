# INSARAG ICMS

- **What:** INSARAG Coordination and Management System — the international USAR coordination stack: Esri/ArcGIS + Survey123 forms, dashboards surfaced via Virtual OSOCC. Encodes ASR levels 1–5, worksite triage/prioritisation, team tasking, and the INSARAG marking/signalling system.
- **Operator:** UN OCHA / INSARAG network; used by classified international USAR teams.
- **Active in this response:** `[UNVERIFIED — depends on whether classified international USAR teams deployed under INSARAG coordination; Nepal accepted limited foreign assistance (→ sources/2026-08-29--field--geospatial-tools-doc.md, grade E provenance — needs corroboration)]`
- **Data model / API:** Survey123 form schemas + ArcGIS feature services; access restricted to responding teams. Not open.
- **Integration potential:** none for an unaffiliated volunteer team — it's a closed operational system. Its *concepts* (worksite triage, ASR levels, sectorisation) are the vocabulary any rescuer-facing tool should speak.

VERDICT: **AVOID** (as a system to touch) / adopt its concepts — we can't access it, and imitating it badly would confuse; but our terminology and any prioritisation outputs should map cleanly onto ASR levels and worksite triage so they're legible to USAR users.
