# Ushahidi

- **What:** Open-source crowdsourced crisis-mapping platform (report intake via SMS/web/social, moderation, map display). Famous from Haiti 2010.
- **Operator:** Ushahidi (non-profit); anyone can deploy an instance.
- **Active in this response:** `[UNVERIFIED — no deployment for this event known to us]`
- **Data model / API:** REST API, categories/surveys, geolocation per report. Mature.
- **Known limitations (documented honestly):** Haiti-era evaluations found crowdsourced reports arrived faster than they could be verified or acted on; unverified reports consumed responder attention; duplication and rumour amplification were persistent; usefulness depended on a dedicated moderation workforce and a responder who had agreed *in advance* to consume the feed. A deployment without a committed consumer is a message board, not a rescue tool.
- **Integration potential:** if a crowdsourced-intake need is validated AND an official consumer commits, deploying Ushahidi beats writing our own intake platform. That's two large "if"s.

VERDICT: **AVOID** for now — not because the software is bad, but because the precondition (a committed official consumer of crowdsourced reports) is unevidenced in this response.
