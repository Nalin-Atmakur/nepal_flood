# ICRC Restoring Family Links / Family Links Network

- **What:** The Red Cross/Red Crescent Movement's family-tracing system: national societies + ICRC running standardised tracing requests, "safe and well" messaging, and (for major events) a `familylinks.icrc.org` event page. Operates under the Movement's protection mandate with a century of doctrine on handling exactly this data.
- **Operator:** ICRC + Nepal Red Cross Society (in-country lead).
- **Active in this response:** NRCS is a standing responder in Nepal; RFL activation for this event `[UNVERIFIED — check familylinks.icrc.org]`.
- **Data model / API:** closed, deliberately — tracing data is protection data. No public API, by design.
- **Integration potential:** none technically, total operationally: RFL is *the* authoritative lane for missing-person tracing. Our job with families who come to us is referral to RFL/NRCS and official police channels, not intake.

VERDICT: **AVOID** (duplicating) — an amateur system that duplicates RFL endangers the people it means to help: it collects protection-grade data without protection-grade safeguards, mandate, or staying power, and splits families' reports away from the channel that can actually act on them.
