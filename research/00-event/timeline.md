# Event & response timeline

*Newest first. Every figure is date-stamped; do not quote any number from here without its date. Compiled 2026-08-29 from sources in `research/sources/` — see refs.*

## 2026-08-29 (day 4)

- as of 10:00 NPT, NDRRMA reported **626 dead, 2,426 missing, 4,400+ rescued**. District deaths: Chitwan 233, Nawalparasi East 158, Nuwakot 51, Gorkha 48, Nawalparasi West 47, Dhading 45, Tanahun 31, Rasuwa 13. (→ sources/2026-08-29--ndrrma-ani--daily-bulletin.md)
- "Missing" rose from ~1,924 (28 Aug evening) to 2,426 — consistent with new report registration, not new casualties; a large share of "missing" reflects downed telecoms and unreconciled lists, not confirmed deaths.
- Weather hampering air operations as rescue enters day 4 (AP, headline-level). Copernicus EMSR927 grading for Bharatpur (AOI04) expected ~17:00 UTC. (→ sources/2026-08-29--copernicus-ems--emsr927.md)
- Aggregator report (single-source, **UNVERIFIED**) of a second, larger upstream blockage still filling. See `secondary-flood-risk.md`.

## 2026-08-28 (day 3)

- Barrier lake at the Chhochen Khola–Purepu Tsangpo confluence **breached**; Nepali Army informed the Rasuwa CDO; NDRRMA confirmed a Bhote Koshi surge; **rescue operations suspended ~3 hours** while people moved to higher ground; observed rise ~0.6 m; operations resumed after China assessed overflow as manageable (lake level later reported down ~10 m). (→ sources/2026-08-28--kathmandu-post--barrier-lake-breach.md, sources/2026-08-28--al-jazeera--two-lakes-explainer.md)
- 350 people rescued alive from the Upper Trishuli hydropower tunnel; 100+ still inside; further workers out of contact at Rasuwagadhi HPP (~100+), Upper Trishuli 3A (~40–45), 3B (~20–25). (→ sources/2026-08-29--kathmandu-post--hydropower-tunnels.md)
- 7 military + 7 civilian helicopters flew 200+ sorties, ~900 people rescued this day. National Rescue Coordination Centre strengthened under cabinet-minister oversight.
- as of ~19:00, Nepali authorities: 579 dead, 1,924 missing (SCMP). BBC live: 616+ dead. Police (11:22): ~475 dead. Conflicting same-day figures — expected during surge registration. (→ sources/2026-08-29--wikipedia--event-aggregate.md)

## 2026-08-27 (day 2)

- China's Ministry of Water Resources warned the barrier lake held ~2,000,000 m³, already overflowing, high breach risk, ~3,000,000 m³ more inflow projected within three days. (→ sources/2026-08-27--kathmandu-post--barrier-lake-warning.md)
- Vantor released open VHR imagery (13 scenes); Copernicus EMSR927 delivered first grading product (Syabrubesi, 19:04 UTC); UNOSAT published mudflow-extent maps for Rasuwa and Nuwakot; HOT launched Tasking Manager projects + uMap.
- Nepal Tourism Board / Dept. of Tourism: 667–668 tourists from 34 countries missing (incl. 127 Nepalis). Chinese officials: 558 missing in Gyirong incl. 260 foreign nationals (earlier figure 265 — more than doubled as registration proceeded).

## 2026-08-26 (day 1 — the event)

- ~08:37 NPT (02:52 UTC): a glacier + bedrock section (~600 m wide, at ~5,200 m; mass estimated 100–200 million m³ by an ICIMOD specialist) collapsed ~1,200 m into the Lhende Khola (Gyirong County, Nepal–Tibet border). USGS registered Ms 5.2 *generated by the collapse itself*; GFZ recorded Mw 5.7; a second ~Ms 4.2 landslide signal ~3 hours later. Debris briefly dammed the Lhende Khola; the dam failed. (→ sources/2026-08-28--kathmandu-post--trigger-reconstruction.md)
- ~7 minutes after the seismic event the surge destroyed Gyirong (Kerung) Port; hit Rasuwagadhi/Timure ~08:40 NPT; ran ~72 km down the Bhote Koshi–Trishuli; bodies later recovered up to ~240 km downstream in Uttar Pradesh, India.
- Same day: ICIMOD preliminary assessment warned of secondary impoundment hazards; DHM requested Sentinel Asia emergency observation (escalated to International Charter activation 1052); Copernicus EMSR927 triggered 09:53 UTC; evacuation orders at Gyirong port. (→ sources/2026-08-26--icimod--preliminary-assessment.md, sources/2026-08-26--sentinel-asia--emergency-observation.md)
- Early tolls that day: at least 165 dead (Al Jazeera) — now superseded; kept to show how fast figures move.

## Context

- The 91 m Miteri "Friendship" Bailey bridge destroyed at Rasuwagadhi was itself a replacement installed Dec 2025 after the **July 2025 flood** destroyed the original — this corridor has now been hit twice in 13 months.
