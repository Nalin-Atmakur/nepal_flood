# Geography

## River system (top → bottom)

```
Glacier collapse site (~5,200 m, Lhende Khola headwaters, Gyirong County, Tibet AR)
  └─ Lhende Khola  ──(border)── Gyirong/Kerung Port (China) · Rasuwagadhi + Timure (Nepal)
       └─ Bhote Koshi (Rasuwa district)  — Syabrubesi
            └─ Trishuli River — Dhunche side valleys, Mailung, Simle, Betrawati (Rasuwa/Nuwakot line)
                 └─ Trishuli/Battar, Bidur (Nuwakot HQ), Galchhi (Dhading), Malekhu
                      └─ joins Narayani system — Devghat, Bharatpur (Chitwan), Nawalparasi
                           └─ Gandak → India (bodies recovered to ~240 km downstream, UP)
```

- Flood run inside Nepal ~72 km of destructive surge; affected districts: Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha, Tanahun, Chitwan, Nawalparasi (E & W). Deaths so far concentrated *downstream* (Chitwan, Nawalparasi) — see timeline district table — likely reflecting population density along the lower Trishuli/Narayani. (→ sources/2026-08-29--ndrrma-ani--daily-bulletin.md)
- Key point locations: Rasuwagadhi border post ≈ **28.2797°N, 85.3777°E**; barrier lake ≈ Chhochen Khola–Purepu Tsangpo confluence, just north of the border.

## Access reality (as of 2026-08-29)

- Betrawati–Rasuwagadhi road (~42 km): washed away. 41 bridges destroyed/damaged; all bridges Trishuli↔Betrawati gone; three concrete bridges Galchhi↔Malekhu gone. Upper corridor settlements are **helicopter-access-only**. (→ sources/2026-08-29--kathmandu-post--bridges-choppers.md)
- Helicopter staging: Dhunche (Rasuwa); coordination nerve centre at Nepali Army barracks, Trishuli, Nuwakot.
- Telecoms: 87 towers down (≈60 NTC + 27 Ncell) across Rasuwa/Nuwakot/Dhading, compounded by grid power loss; joint emergency-comms team restoring repeaters (Nyaupanechaur, Ramche) by airlifted generators. (→ sources/2026-08-27--onlinekhabar--telecom-status.md)
- Hydropower: ~430 MW operating capacity knocked out across 14–15 Trishuli-corridor projects; tunnels became refuge/trap sites (see timeline).
