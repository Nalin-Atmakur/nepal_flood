# Advocacy & media note

*Compiled 2026-08-29. The user-stated motivation: "rescuers seem to be travel agencies and the government using armies — we can push policy/media outlets so people are aware." What the evidence supports, and the rules.*

## First, correct the premise slightly

The response is larger than it looks from abroad: 13,000+ security personnel, 14–15 helicopters (military + three named civilian operators), Chinese teams with drones and engineering assets, EMSR927/Charter/Sentinel Asia space assets, an NDRRMA drone pipeline, and an IFRC CHF 25M appeal (→ research/10-actors/actors.md). Travel agencies are visible because they trace their own clients — a real gap-filler for the transient population, not the whole response. Advocacy built on "nobody is responding" would be factually wrong and would burn credibility. Advocacy built on the *systemic* gaps below is well-evidenced.

## The evidence-backed asks

1. **Cross-border early warning for the HKH.** This corridor flooded catastrophically **two years running** (Jul 2025 GLOF, ~29 deaths; Aug 2026, 626+ as of 29 Aug) — and both sources were lakes that static risk inventories had not flagged (→ sources/2026-05--the-innovation--glof-prediction-commentary.md). Upstream early-warning stations were themselves swept away before alerts went out. ICIMOD is already publicly calling for cross-border early warning — amplifying an existing institutional ask is the strongest, safest policy lane.
2. **Family-information coherence.** Families walk hospital noticeboards in the rain while official lists diverge and private PII tables circulate (→ sources/2026-08-29--multiple--missing-lists-fragmentation.md). The ask: resource the official channels (Police DB, RFL) and consolidate public communication of *where families should go* — not new platforms.
3. **The misinformation flood.** Five+ fact-check desks debunked AI-generated fake footage within 48 h (→ sources/2026-08-28--fact-checkers--ai-fake-videos.md). The ask to media: publish verification anchors (USGS/ICIMOD/CEMS products) alongside coverage; the ask to platforms: label recycled/synthetic disaster content fast.
4. **Fund the appeal, don't self-deploy.** Kathmandu Post's own how-to-help guidance warns against self-deployment and unverified fundraisers; the PM Disaster Relief Fund and the IFRC appeal are the legitimate funding lanes.

## Audiences and channels

- International outlets already covering (Guardian/BBC/AP/Al Jazeera live pages) — pitch the *early-warning two-years-running* angle with ICIMOD/USGS sourcing; it's underreported relative to casualty counts.
- Diaspora networks — signal-boost official family channels (familylinks.icrc.org, Police DB, embassy lines), not private lists.
- Tech/OSM communities — recruit for the HOT campaign's actual deficits (validators; lower-corridor mapping), with the published coordination contact.

## Hard rules (from research/50-ethics-and-legal/DO_NO_HARM.md)

- Never publish casualty/missing figures of our own; quote only `as of <date>, <source>`.
- Never amplify imagery without provenance; check the fact-check desks first.
- Never second-guess operational decisions (e.g. the rescue pause during the breach) in public.
- Never present volunteer analysis as official information; label everything.
- Never link, mirror, or amplify PII lists — including "helpfully" pointing families at private trackers.
