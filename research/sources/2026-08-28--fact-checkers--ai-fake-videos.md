# Fact-check roundup: AI-generated and recycled fake flood footage

## Summary

Multiple independent fact-check desks documented fake content flooding social media within 48 hours: AI-generated videos (including one purporting to show a missile destroying a dam and "causing" the flood — ~1.8M views on one Facebook post), a staged video of women posing as dead misrepresented as victims, and genuine old footage from Japan and India recycled with false captions.

Other URLs: verafiles.org (missile/dam debunk), hoax-alert.leadstories.com (AI video; staged video), yahoo.com/news (AFP debunk), dfrac.org 2026-08-27 (viral AI video).

## Extracted claims

- as of 2026-08-27/28: at least five independent desks published debunks of AI-generated or recycled footage about this event.
- Related: Tibet-side videos scrubbed from Chinese platforms; foreign journalists denied access — Tibet information flows only through state media (NYT/BBC/Guardian via Wikipedia).
