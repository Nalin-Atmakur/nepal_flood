# Sentinel Asia emergency observation — Rasuwa flash flood

## Summary

Same-day emergency observation activation requested by Nepal's DHM, escalated to International Charter activation 1052. Space agencies tasked SAR and optical; value-added products already published.

## Extracted claims

- Tasked/contributed: JAXA ALOS-2 SAR (Jan 2026 pre-event baseline), ISRO Cartosat-1 archive, Resourcesat-2A (Apr 2026), EOS-04 SAR acquired 26 Aug 2026.
- VAPs as of 28 Aug: IWM analysis of Planet imagery (JPG+ZIP); **EOS-RS Sentinel-1 damage proxy maps (PNG + KMZ)** — downloadable without auth; raw tasked imagery mostly restricted to authorised requesters.
- Damage proxy maps have known false positives in steep terrain; VAPs are JPG/PNG/KMZ, not analysis-ready vectors.

## Relevance

- 60: EOS-RS damage proxy (KMZ→GeoJSON) is the fastest all-weather "check here" layer where cloud blocks optical.
