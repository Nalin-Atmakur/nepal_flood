# Telecom status: 87 towers down

## Summary

87 telecom towers down (~60 Nepal Telecom + 27 Ncell) across Rasuwa, Nuwakot and Dhading, compounded by grid power loss. A joint emergency-communications team (MoIC, MoHA, Army, Police, NTA, NTC, Ncell) was stood up; NTC airlifted generators/batteries to Nyaupanechaur and Ramche repeater sites (restoration progress per ICT Frame, ~28-29 Aug).

## Extracted claims

- The "missing" count partly reflects downed telecoms — settlements out of contact, not confirmed losses; counts moved as coverage was restored.
