# HOT/NAXA Open Drone Aerial Tasking Manager

## Summary

HOT and NAXA describe DroneTM, an open digital public good intended to coordinate local drone pilots and reduce fragmented or redundant aerial collection. The project was motivated partly by Nepal's 2021 Melamchi flood, when roads were blocked, helicopters could not fly and local authorities needed rapid drone assessment. DroneTM is therefore the existing system to investigate before creating any new drone-flight request or coverage-tasking application.

## Extracted claims

- DroneTM exists to coordinate drone collection and create usable high-resolution imagery for disaster response.
- NAXA is a core development partner with Nepal disaster-mapping experience.
- The source establishes product intent and prior testing, not confirmed deployment in the August 2026 response.

## Contradictions

- The Nepal government opened a Google Form for this event rather than publicly directing pilots to DroneTM. Whether DroneTM is active behind the intake is unknown.

## Relevance

- 20-existing-systems: avoid a new drone tasking platform; investigate a narrow intake/QA adapter instead.
