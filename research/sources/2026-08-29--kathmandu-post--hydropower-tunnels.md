# Hundreds feared trapped in hydropower tunnels

## Summary

Hydropower tunnels became both refuges and traps. ~430 MW knocked out across 14–15 Trishuli-corridor projects (Rasuwagadhi 111 MW, Chilime, Upper Trishuli 3A/3B, Trishuli, Devighat); ~470 MW under construction also damaged.

## Extracted claims

- as of 2026-08-28: 350 rescued alive from the Upper Trishuli tunnel (corroborated by Radio Nepal, Nepal Press); 100+ still inside.
- as of 2026-08-28/29: out of contact — Rasuwagadhi HPP ~100+, Upper Trishuli 3A ~40–45, 3B ~20–25.
- Conflicting "191 rescued from a hydropower tunnel" also circulated — likely different tunnel/date; UNRESOLVED.
