# Disaster imagery viewer — nepal-floods-2026

## Summary

Browser swipe/compare viewer federating live services over the corridor: Sentinel-1/-2 via Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC (self-updating as new passes land), OpenAerialMap (incl. Vantor), Esri basemap, live OSM via HOT Raw Data API with post-event edits highlighted, TM grids, UNOSAT flood extent. Hosts no data of its own; no auth.

## Extracted claims

- Effectively self-updating for satellite layers; viewer shell maintained by one person (availability risk).
- Linked from the official activation wiki as the activation's viewer.
