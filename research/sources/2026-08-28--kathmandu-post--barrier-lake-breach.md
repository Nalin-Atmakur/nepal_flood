# Barrier lake breach (2026-08-28)

## Summary

The barrier lake breached on 28 Aug. The warning chain ran Nepali Army → Rasuwa CDO; NDRRMA confirmed a Bhote Koshi surge. Nepal monitoring: helicopter recce from Dhunche, NDRRMA river-level watch. Residents had already been moved from vulnerable riverside areas after the first flood. A national Rescue Coordination Centre was strengthened under cabinet-minister oversight.

## Extracted claims

- as of 2026-08-28: breach confirmed; rescue suspended ~3 hours (corroborated by NepalNews/Al Jazeera); observed rise ~0.6 m; operations resumed after overflow assessed manageable; Chinese state media later reported the lake level down ~10 m, risk "gradually decreasing".
