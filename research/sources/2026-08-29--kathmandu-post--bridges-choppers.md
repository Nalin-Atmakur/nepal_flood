# Washed-out bridges force communities onto helicopters

## Summary

Access status day 4: ~40–42 km of the Betrawati–Rasuwagadhi road destroyed; 41 bridges destroyed/damaged; all bridges between Trishuli and Betrawati gone; three concrete bridges Galchhi–Malekhu gone; Phurke Khola bridge (Dhading) gone. Upper-corridor communities depend entirely on helicopters. Infrastructure damage estimated Rs 20 billion (roads/bridges scope; a separate Rs 200B+ total-property figure circulates — different scopes).
