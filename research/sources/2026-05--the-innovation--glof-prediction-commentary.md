# Zhang et al. 2026 — Challenges in predicting glacial lake outburst floods

## Summary

Commentary (The Innovation 7(5), DOI 10.1016/j.xinn.2026.101349, open access). Note: **the ScienceDirect link the team circulated (PII S2666675826000962) is this same article.** Synthesises why GLOF prediction fails: different lake types fail via different triggers (ice avalanche, landslide, extreme rain, permafrost thaw, piping); minor disturbances cascade; timely warnings remain rare even where early-warning systems exist. Its anchor case is the **2025-07-08 China–Nepal transboundary GLOF in Jilong/Gyirong County (~29 deaths) — the previous year's flood on this same Bhote Koshi corridor — sourced from a small supraglacial lake that risk inventories had not flagged.**

## Extracted claims

- Static "dangerous lake" inventories (e.g. the 47 PDGLs flagged by ICIMOD/UNDP 2020) did not flag the source of either the 2025 or 2026 event.
- Direct lesson: monitor the *actually formed* barrier lakes with imagery; don't rely on susceptibility maps.
