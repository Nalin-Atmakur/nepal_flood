# Planet open disaster data release — nepal-flash-flood-2026-08-26

## Summary

Free open release for this event: 24 scenes across 3 sensors — PlanetScope ~3.8 m (19), SkySat ~0.80 m (2), Pelican ~0.55 m (3). Pre-event baseline 2026-05-27; post-event 2026-08-26/28. Coverage: Trishuli Bazaar → Betrawati → Dhunche → Syabrubesi → Rasuwagadhi → Gyirong. STAC catalog, COGs, GeoParquet indices, usability masks. Licence CC-BY-NC-4.0; no account needed.

## Extracted claims

- Planet's own caveats: heavy monsoon cloud throughout; clear-fraction metadata unreliable; Pelican usability masks misclassify steep shadowed terrain.
- The 0.55–0.8 m post scenes are the best open optical data for damaged-building / blocked-road interpretation, complementing Vantor.
- The 2026-05-27 pre-event baseline is only 3 months old — much fresher than most Vantor pre scenes.

## Relevance

- 60: primary imagery input for change detection and the elevation/reachability work.
