# Kathmandu Post: scientific reconstruction of the trigger

## Summary

Scientists' reconstruction: a glacier + bedrock section ~600 m wide at ~5,200 m collapsed ~1,200 m into the Lhende Khola; debris temporarily dammed the river; the dam failed, releasing the surge. Corroborated by Planet imagery. USGS: the Ms 5.2 seismic signal was generated by the collapse itself (not a preceding quake); GFZ recorded Mw 5.7 plus a ~Ms 4.2 second landslide ~3 h later. DHM confirmed analysis using Chinese satellite data.

## Extracted claims

- Collapse ~08:37 NPT (02:52 UTC) 2026-08-26; surge destroyed Gyirong Port ~7 minutes later; hit Rasuwagadhi/Timure ~08:40.
- Collapsed mass estimate 100–200 million m³ (ICIMOD specialist).

## Contradictions

- Wikipedia places the collapse "near Langtang Lirung"; KP/ICIMOD place it in Lhende Khola headwaters on the Gyirong side. Peak identity `[UNVERIFIED]`; valley consistent.
