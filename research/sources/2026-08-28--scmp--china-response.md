# SCMP: China's response and barrier-lake engineering

## Summary

Chinese engineering teams are conducting aerial drone surveys with portable satellite stations and 3D laser scanners to build 3D models of the debris barrier, plus flood simulations and hydrological analysis. Politburo pledged urgent emergency assistance to Nepal; top-level order for "all-out" search. Tibet-side response: 681 firefighters, 47 drones, 13 dogs (27 Aug evening), PLA + Sichuan teams (via NBC/Xinhua).
