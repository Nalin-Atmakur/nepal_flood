# Government opens drone-imagery intake form

## Summary

On 29 Aug the government opened a Google Form for drone pilots, operators, organisations and media to submit aerial footage/photos/mapping data from affected areas. Submissions are verified and passed to NDRRMA and other agencies — explicitly to centralise data and avoid redundant collection.
