# ICIMOD preliminary assessment (same-day)

## Summary

Same-day scientific assessment: an ice-rock avalanche delivered a large volume of ice and rock into the Lhende Khola, forming a landslide dam whose failure caused the flood; warned of secondary hazards from further impounded water; documented gauge surges (9 m in 30 min at Galchhi; 7 m at Malekhu); evacuation ordered at Gyirong port. ICIMOD has a direct line to Nepal's PMO and Chinese institutions and is analysing hydrological/seismic/remote-sensing data; notes cloud cover limits optical observation; calls for cross-border early warning.

## Extracted claims

- as of 2026-08-26, ICIMOD: trigger = ice-rock avalanche → landslide dam → dam failure (cause unconfirmed at that time).
- An ICIMOD senior cryosphere specialist later estimated the collapsed mass at 100–200 million m³ (via Kathmandu Post 2026-08-28).
