# Road and telecom restoration update

## Summary

Radio Nepal relayed a prime-ministerial update that Rasuwa had regained a road connection through the Bogatitar–Kalikasthan–Dhunche alternative route and one-way traffic had begun on the Kathmandu–Mugling road. It also reported that 145 of 198 affected telecom sites had resumed service. The same update said helicopter allocation was considering air safety, geographic access and assessed need.

## Extracted claims

- As of 2026-08-29, an alternative road route to Rasuwa had opened after the district had been disconnected from the national road network.
- As of 2026-08-29, 145 of 198 affected Nepal Telecom/Ncell sites were reported operational again.
- Helicopter decisions were being constrained by air safety, geography and assessed need.

## Contradictions

- This rapid restoration update does not contradict the extensive bridge and main-road destruction reported the same day. It demonstrates why a static access layer becomes stale within hours.
