# HOT/NAXA Field Tasking Manager

## Summary

Field-TM is an existing open-source fieldwork coordinator built by HOT and NAXA around ODK Collect. It assigns bounded areas or features, supports Android field collection and is designed to reduce missed coverage, duplicate visits, incomplete submissions and difficult data cleaning. It has been tested in Nepal. This overlaps almost exactly with a generic "which places have teams visited?" app.

## Extracted claims

- Field-TM explicitly addresses field assignment, missed areas, duplicate visits, completeness/quality and private/public-data separation.
- It uses ODK Collect and commonly available Android devices.
- HOT operates a public instance, but the source warns that the product is beta and requires an ODK Central setup for project use.
- Deployment in the August 2026 flood response is unverified.

## Contradictions

- None. The open question is adoption for this response, not existence.
