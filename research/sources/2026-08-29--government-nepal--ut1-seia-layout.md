# Public Upper Trishuli-1 project-layout material

## Summary

The public supplementary environmental assessment contains project-area maps and an optimised-design description for Upper Trishuli-1. It identifies the headrace and pressure tunnels, powerhouse and tailrace, surge shaft, several adits, bridges and internal access roads. Crucially, it documents design changes: one adit was cancelled, others were shifted, and some access-road plans changed. Its March 2025 progress table also distinguishes completed and proposed components.

The document demonstrates that a rescue worksite pack can be bootstrapped from public material, but also why it must be confirmed against the plant operator's current as-built drawings. A stale EIA diagram must never be presented as an operationally verified plan.

## Extracted claims

- The document contains labelled layouts for the headworks, powerhouse and project facilities, plus descriptions of adits, portals, bridges and access roads.
- The optimised design moved Adit 1 and Adit 4, cancelled Adit 2 and retained Adit 3, so source date and design version are safety-critical.
- A March 2025 progress table describes several main underground components as incomplete while Adit 3 was complete.
- Project access includes steep and landslide-prone segments; the document itself describes high-risk slopes and changed access arrangements.

## Contradictions

- Public reporting sometimes conflates Upper Trishuli-1, Upper Trishuli 3A and Upper Trishuli 3B. This document applies only to UT-1. Any rescue packet must use a unique worksite identifier and must not mix plans between projects.
