# Al Jazeera: is Nepal facing another flood from a new lake?

## Summary

Describes **two impoundments**: an upper lake inside China where meltwater pooled, and the lower barrier lake at the Chhochen Khola–Purepu Tsangpo confluence. Expert assessments: a Tribhuvan University water engineer warned of sudden dam failure given loose rock/mud composition; a glacial-flood researcher warned a breach could mobilise sediment, hamper rescue, and threaten hydropower tunnel workers. Also records international aid (India 10 t supplies, UN CERF $2M, Australia $3.6M).
