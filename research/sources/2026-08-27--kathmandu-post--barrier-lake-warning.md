# China's barrier-lake warning (2026-08-27)

## Summary

China's Ministry of Water Resources warned of a debris-dammed lake at the Chhochen Khola–Purepu Tsangpo confluence.

## Extracted claims

- as of 2026-08-27 morning, China MWR: lake ~2,000,000 m³, already overflowing, high breach risk; ~3,000,000 m³ additional inflow projected over the following three days (to ~Aug 30).
