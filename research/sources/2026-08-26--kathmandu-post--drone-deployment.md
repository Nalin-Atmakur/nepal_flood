# Drones deployed to search for survivors

## Summary

The Nepal Drone Association mobilised 50+ pilots and data specialists to Rasuwa, Nuwakot and Dhading with thermal cameras (survivor location) and LiDAR (debris-depth assessment), operating under a pre-existing agreement with NDRRMA signed 15 July. NagarikAlert also deployed thermal drones (OnlineKhabar). The government publicly appealed for drone photos/videos from affected areas (Khabarhub, 29 Aug). Drone/satellite imagery is being posted to OpenAerialMap; Youth Innovation Lab drone field assessments feed NESRA FloodWatch (nesraspace.org/floodwatch/rasuwa-2026/).
