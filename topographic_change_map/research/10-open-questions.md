# Open questions

## Scope and accuracy

- What are the exact coordinates and boundary of `syabrubesi-pilot-v1`?
- Is Rasuwagadhi still an active target or only previous research context?
- What is the smallest change we need to detect?
- Are broad deposition classes acceptable, or is metre-scale per-building measurement required?
- What validation threshold determines operational versus research-only use?

## Planet

- Do the exact SkySat and Pelican acquisitions have Basic products?
- Are RPCs embedded, separate, or unavailable for these products?
- Does the account have search, activation, and download entitlement?
- Do both products overlap the exact pilot AOI?
- What is the ray-derived convergence angle across that overlap?
- Is the approximately four-hour time gap acceptable given flood and water movement?
- Can the two sensors be radiometrically normalized well enough for dense matching?

## Vantor

- Where are the reproducible artifacts from the reported feature-matching test?
- What exact AOI produced approximately 16% sparse support?
- What transformation was removed before residual parallax was measured?
- Can original WorldView products and camera models be obtained through a partner?
- Do the public opposite-look footprints overlap the exact current AOI, not just the general corridor?

## Pre-flood baseline

- Does recent pre-flood stereo exist with original camera models?
- Can Airbus, Planet, Vantor, or Charter partners provide it?
- Is historical Cartosat-1 good enough for any declared use?
- What terrain changes occurred between an older baseline and August 2026?
- Are surveyed, drone, or engineering elevation data available for validation?

## Alignment and validation

- Which areas can be independently classified as stable terrain?
- Are both surfaces referenced to the same vertical datum?
- What horizontal error remains after co-registration?
- How does residual elevation error vary with slope and aspect?
- What independent elevation controls are available?
- How will uncertainty be propagated into per-building summaries?

## Interpretation

- How will roofs, vegetation, rubble, and exposed ground be distinguished?
- What evidence is required before positive surface change is called deposition?
- What evidence is required before deposition is translated into building burial?
- Who is qualified to review and approve operational interpretation?

## Product and governance

- Who is the intended user of the change map?
- Is the product research-only, planning-support, or intended for operational decisions?
- Which imagery licences allow derived products to be shared publicly?
- Which artifacts can be stored in Git, and which must remain in controlled object storage?
- Who approves publication and confidence wording?
